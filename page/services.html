What You Can Do with Usefooma

Usefooma (formerly Pulsepoint) is an all-in-one WordPress plugin for digital services, bill payments, and fintech operations.

Built for entrepreneurs, agencies, and digital businesses to sell airtime, data bundles, electricity tokens, cable TV subscriptions, eSIMs, virtual cards, and cryptocurrency services directly from their WordPress websites.

Digital Service Automation

Automate airtime, data, electricity bills, cable TV, and eSIM delivery directly from your WordPress dashboard.

Wallet & Payment System

Integrated wallet system with Paystack, Monnify, and other gateways for seamless deposits and transactions.

Reseller & Growth Tools

Enable reseller systems, user management, and marketing tools to scale your digital business faster.
